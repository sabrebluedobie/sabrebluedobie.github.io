About Bluedobie Developing

I'm Melanie Brown. I build small websites for people who need them done right, without the wait or the agency price tag.

What I Actually Do

I build landing pages, portfolio sites, and simple business websites for solopreneurs and small businesses. Website builds come in under $3k. Fast turnaround. No 6-month timelines.

I'm based in Princeton, Kentucky, serving Western KY and beyond. You work directly with me - no account managers, no committees, no agency overhead.

Most website projects take 2-4 weeks from start to launch. You get a functional, mobile-friendly site that actually works.

I also do a few things that aren't websites — a free audit if you want a diagnosis first, focused AI and logo reviews, and the DobieCore content system. They're all the same method: something practical, with a defined edge, sized for a real small business. See how it's grouped.

Is This a Fit?

I'd rather tell you no early than take on a project I can't do well. Here is the honest edge of the work.

A good fit

  • Solopreneurs and small businesses that need a real site, not the template everyone else is using
  • Landing pages, portfolios, and simple business sites — a handful of pages, not dozens
  • Website budgets under $3k, launching in 2-4 weeks
  • Existing sites that have gone broken, confusing, or slow
  • Owners who want to talk to the person actually building the thing

Outside what I take on

  • Large e-commerce builds and custom web applications
  • Six-month projects and open-ended retainers
  • Work that needs an account manager, a committee, or agency process
  • Running your marketing for you — I build the site and the systems, you run the business

If your project lands on this side, I'll tell you early and point you somewhere better instead of taking it on.

How I Got Here

My dad taught me DOS as a kid on a computer he built from scratch. We moved around a lot (military family), and technology was the constant. I watched him build things, and I wanted to do the same.

I'm entirely self-taught. No bootcamp, no CS degree - just figuring things out, breaking things, fixing them. I learned from Google, University of Michigan courses, and years of trial and error.

Bluedobie Developing is named after my blue Doberman, Sabre. He's been my office companion through every project.

Why Bluedobie Is Adding Content Systems

Websites were never the whole problem. Most small business owners I work with also struggle with content — what to post, how to sound consistent, and how to keep up without it eating their week.

That's real overwhelm, not a lack of effort. Time is limited, content needs are constant, and most tools out there assume you have more of both than you actually do.

That's why DobieCore Suite exists: a content system built on your brand voice, so you're not starting from a blank page every time. It's built the same way I build websites — practical, plain-language, and sized for a real small business, not a marketing department.

DobieCore is now being combined with Bifrost, a context and workflow-routing layer, into one system — DobieCore + Bifrost. Same goal: fewer scattered decisions and more clear next steps, with the business context living somewhere other than your head. DobieCore Suite works today; the rest is being built.
