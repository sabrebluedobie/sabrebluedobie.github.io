Frequently Asked Questions

The questions people actually ask before hiring me.

Website Projects

What does a 3-page website build include?
Three pages — usually Home, Services, and Contact — with a responsive layout that works on phones, on-brand colors and type, fast load times, basic on-page SEO setup, and a simple path for making edits later. Need more pages than that? Say so in your inquiry and it gets quoted as part of the scope. See what's in each service.
What do I need to provide?
Your logo and any brand colors you already use, the words and photos you want on each page, and a clear idea of what you want a visitor to do — call, book, buy, or get in touch. If you don't have the copy or photos ready yet, say so up front. That changes the timeline, not the answer.
How long does a website project take?
Most small website projects take 2-4 weeks from start to launch, depending on scope and how quickly content comes back. You get a clear estimate before any work starts. See how the process works.
What does a website cost?
Small business website builds are priced under $3k, quoted after a short conversation about what you actually need — no surprise invoices. Updates, edits, and cleanup are quoted separately as their own work. The Logo Sprint is the one service with published tiers, starting at $199.
Who owns the website after launch?
You do. It's your site, and you keep control of it — you review, we launch, and it's yours. Where you want the domain and hosting in your own accounts, that's how it gets set up; if you'd rather I hold a piece of it, ask and we'll scope that explicitly rather than leaving it vague.
Can you update the site later?
Yes. Website Updates & Light Edits is a standing service booked by appointment — content swaps, image updates, accessibility fixes, and small changes without a full rebuild. There's no retainer and no monthly minimum; you come back when you need something. If a site has drifted into broken, confusing, or slow, that's Website Cleanup instead. See both post-launch options.
Do you only work with businesses in Western Kentucky?
No. I'm based in Princeton, Kentucky and work with clients everywhere — website projects run remotely. Western Kentucky is home, so local clients can meet in person, and DobieCore Suite is available nationally to any small business.
Ready to talk about a build?
Tell me what you need and I'll come back with a quote. Start a website project inquiry.

Free Web Audit

Do I need a call to request the free audit?
No. The request form starts the audit — pick Free Web Audit and include your website address. A call is optional later if it would actually help. The booking calendar on the Contact page is for consultations, not for audit requests, so you don't have to schedule anything to get one. Request the audit.
How does the free web audit work?
It comes back as two reports. First, an automated PageSpeed Insights PDF covering loading performance, mobile experience, accessibility, and technical SEO. Then, a follow-up manual report: search visibility findings, a hands-on walk through your site the way a customer would use it, the hidden blockers that turns up, and recommendations in priority order. See everything the audit covers.
Is the audit really free?
Yes. No cost, and no requirement to book or buy anything afterward. Use the roadmap yourself, hand it to your current developer, or ask me to implement it — all three are fine outcomes.
What if the audit says my site is fine?
Then you've got that in writing and you can stop worrying about it. The audit is meant to tell you what to fix first, and sometimes the honest answer is that the site isn't the problem.

Is This a Fit?

I'm not sure what I need. Where do I start?
If you already have a site and something about it isn't working, start with the free web audit — it's built to tell you which problem to solve first. If you know you need a site built or rebuilt, skip the audit and send a project inquiry instead.
What happens after I send an inquiry?
You hear back within 24 hours. We talk about what you need, you get a clear quote, and if you approve it I build it and you launch it. No account managers and no committees — you work directly with me the whole way.
What if my project is bigger than a small website?
I'll tell you. Building small is a deliberate choice — landing pages, portfolios, and simple business sites, not six-month builds. If you need a large e-commerce or application project, I'd rather point you somewhere else than take it on and do it badly. The free audit is still open to you either way. Read why I work this way.
Do you do anything besides websites?
Yes, and each one is deliberately bounded rather than open-ended: a free web audit if you want a diagnosis first, an AI Setup Review for local service businesses already using ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini, a Logo Sprint for concepts in 48-72 hours, and the DobieCore content system and course for handling content yourself. See all of it grouped by what you're trying to do.

DobieCore + Bifrost

What is DobieCore Suite?
DobieCore Suite is a brand voice-based content system that helps small businesses with prewriting, captions, images, blogs, and a content calendar template — all built from one consistent brand voice. Explore DobieCore + Bifrost.
How much does DobieCore Suite cost?
Signing up is free, and no credit card is required until you pass the free usage limits.
Is DobieCore Suite only for Western Kentucky businesses?
No. DobieCore Suite is available nationally to any small business that wants brand-consistent content support.
What is Bifrost?
Bifrost is the context and routing layer being combined with DobieCore into one system, DobieCore + Bifrost. It covers AI workflow routing and small business continuity planning, including support for unexpected owner absence. The backend runs today; the combined product is still being built, while DobieCore Suite is available now. Read more on the DobieCore + Bifrost page.
Can I sign up for Bifrost now?
Not yet. It will likely start as a local-first test with a small group of Western Kentucky businesses. If you're curious, reach out and we'll keep you posted.
What is DobieCore Learn?
A course for working with AI clearly, whichever assistant you already use — three modules and 36 lessons that end with a reusable business foundation instead of a folder of someone else's prompts. See what the course covers.
