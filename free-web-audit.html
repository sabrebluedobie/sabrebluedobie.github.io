How it works

Your Three-Part Website Audit

Each part answers a different question about how well your website supports your business.

1

Technical & Performance Report

We run an automated Google PageSpeed Insights audit to measure the technical side of your site.

  • Loading performance and Core Web Vitals
  • Mobile experience and best practices
  • Accessibility checks
  • Technical SEO fundamentals

You receive this report first as a PDF, with priority issues and practical next steps.

2

Search Visibility Review

Using Ahrefs' free tools where appropriate, we look for signals that may affect how customers find you online.

  • Basic site health and crawl concerns
  • Broken links and backlink signals
  • Keyword and content opportunities
  • Search visibility gaps worth investigating

We translate useful findings into plain language instead of burying you in marketing metrics.

3

Customer Journey & Conversion Review

Then we use your website the way a real customer would and manually look for hidden blockers.

  • Do phone and email buttons reach the right place?
  • Do contact forms, booking tools, social links, and calls to action work?
  • Can visitors quickly understand what you offer and what to do next?
  • Do pricing, messaging, navigation, or missing trust signals create hesitation?

This is where we catch the things businesses often build once and understandably forget to retest.
