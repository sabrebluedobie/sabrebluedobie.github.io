Most AI courses teach you what to type

Prompt packs stop working the moment your question changes. You're left with a folder of someone else's words and no idea why they worked — or why they suddenly don't.

DobieCore Learn teaches the part that transfers: how to describe your business, your customer, your voice, and your limits clearly enough that AI stops guessing. The Golden Rule of the whole course is simple — treat AI the way you'd train a new employee. Be specific, give examples, ask follow-up questions, review the work, refine together.

AI should amplify your voice, not replace it.

If you finish this course and feel like you can talk to AI without buying anyone's prompt pack again, it worked.
